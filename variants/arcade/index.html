About

I'm a UC Irvine Computer Science student who likes taking ML systems all the way from data prep to a thing people can actually run. My research with the Aspiring Scholars Directed Research Program (ASDRP) built a video-based American Sign Language to English translation system in Python — published as first author in the 2025 IEEE International Conference on Consumer Electronics (ICCE) proceedings.

Since then I've focused on local-first AI: fine-tuning a 4-bit Qwen3-4B model with QLoRA on Apple MLX with a side-by-side evaluation UI, and shipping a Windows app that records Zoom classes and transcribes them offline with Whisper. At Leopard Imaging I prototyped stereo-camera depth estimation for real-time image processing; at Penumbra I supported endpoint deployment and troubleshooting at a medical-device company.
